## RARE ANIMAL SPECIES
- This web app is all about the animal species that are rare to find in the whole world but they are located in different continent around the globe.

---
# Why this web app?
- Some of the people interested in nature and wildlife have a difficult time locating this different species of animals around the world,and they have to spend more time researching and locating this species. But on this website they ease their time in doing more research.
---

## FEATURES
* Being able to locate their habitats.
* Some are unable to be located because of their frequent migration.
* location of some of the species.
* Their value,this means whether they are : 'endangered,critically endangered or vulnarable.'
* Authentication for the website using FireBase.
